# S-09 · Policy discovery and fetch

## Goal
Locate the privacy policy, cookie policy and terms across common link patterns, footers, and language variants.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Finds the policy on at least 9 of 10 canary sites
- [ ] Handles multi-page policies and per-language variants
- [ ] Records the URL, fetch time and a content hash as evidence
- [ ] A site with no discoverable policy is itself a finding
