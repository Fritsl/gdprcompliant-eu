# P-01 · Roles and scoped task lists

## Goal
Marketing, IT, HR, Finance — each sees only what they can actually change.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Under six items per role, enforced by the assembler rather than by hope
- [ ] Scope is derived from the finding, not assigned manually
- [ ] Every item offers "I do not know, check for me" which routes back to the agent
- [ ] A role member cannot see the rest of the case unless the owner grants it
