# V-03 · Verbatim law, guaranteed

## Goal
Show legal text word for word, and make it structurally impossible for a model to be the thing that typed it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Quoted passages are fetched from the corpus by identifier and rendered without passing through a model
- [x] A rendered quotation is compared character for character against the corpus entry before it is displayed
- [x] Truncation, ellipsis or annotation inside a quotation fails the check rather than degrading quietly
- [x] Every quotation carries instrument, article and paragraph, and the corpus version it came from
- [x] Consolidated text is dated, so an amended article is never shown as current without its date

## How this is verified
```bash
pnpm test:integration -- verbatim && pnpm run check:citations
```

- 2026-09-04 18:10 — A quotation is a contract (QuotationSchema): instrument, article, paragraph and point, the reference as it reads, the whole text, the hash it was cut with, the corpus version, the date the consolidated text speaks from, and where it was read. It is made in exactly one way, quotationOf(chunk), from a chunk fetched by identifier (quotation() over the files, quotationFromStore() over the store), so nothing that writes prose is in the path. verbatim(rendered, quotation) compares character for character and names the failure: hash (the entry was altered after it was cut), ellipsis, annotation, truncated, differs (with the index); excerptOf() places a quoted span inside the whole paragraph and refuses dots and notes of its own. Every corpus instrument now says source.textAsOf (GDPR 2016-05-04, ePrivacy 2009-12-19), the cellar ingest stamps it from sources.json, the store keeps it (migration 0023), and an undated instrument cannot be quoted and fails check:citations. The report's articles are quotations, checked in reportModel and again in reportPdf where they are drawn, with 'Text as of <date>' on every one; the advisor page renders law through the Verbatim component, which takes a citation and never text, marks the quoted span inside the whole paragraph, and stops the page on any failure. tests/integration/verbatim.test.ts covers the files, the store (a row altered after ingest is caught before display), version pinning (an older version answers with its own text and its own date), and the report refusing a shortened, dotted or annotated article.
