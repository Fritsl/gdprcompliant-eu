# A-08 · Corpus retrieval with jurisdiction filter

## Goal
Retrieval over regulation, recitals, guidance and case law that can resolve a citation to an exact paragraph.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every corpus chunk carries jurisdiction, instrument, article and paragraph identifiers
- [ ] A citation string resolves to exactly one paragraph or fails — no fuzzy nearest match
- [ ] Retrieval can be filtered to a jurisdiction and never leaks another country as authority
- [ ] Corpus version is recorded on every claim so old findings stay explicable

## How this is verified
```bash
pnpm test:integration -- corpus-resolve
```
