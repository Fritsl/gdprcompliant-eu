# S-11 · Form inventory and consent patterns

## Goal
Enumerate every form, its fields, its destination, whether a notice sits at the point of collection, and whether opt-in is bundled or pre-ticked.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Detects pre-ticked consent boxes including CSS-hidden and JS-set states
- [ ] Flags bundled consent where one checkbox covers marketing and terms together
- [ ] Classifies field sensitivity so a form asking for health or financial data raises severity
- [ ] Never submits a form — read-only inspection only

```bash

```
