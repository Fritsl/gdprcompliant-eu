# U-05 · Public trust page

## Goal
An opt-in public page a company links from its own site, showing dated progress without overclaiming.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Off by default; publishing is an explicit, reversible act recorded on the timeline
- [x] Wording is "work in progress, dated" and never implies certification or approval
- [x] Shows what has been fixed and when, never the open findings, which are theirs alone
- [x] Carries a scan-your-own-domain call to action — this is the growth loop
