# S-10 · Article 13/14 clause analysis

## Goal
Test the policy clause by clause against the disclosure requirements, with every result carrying the sentence it was drawn from or an explicit not-found.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Model output conforms to the Zod schema in @gc/contracts or the run fails
